---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. Use when the user wants to stress-test their thinking, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding. By default, walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one; a skill that builds on this one may set its own question order. Before providing any recommendation, first consider what best practice applies in the specific context. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

The interview ends when every material question has been resolved and I explicitly confirm that we have reached a shared understanding. Then it is simply over: write no summary, no document, and no next step.

Do not act on the result.
